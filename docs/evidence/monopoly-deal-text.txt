      WHAT THE GAME’S ABOUT
               Monopoly Deal is all about collecting properties and stealing from your
                 opponents! Use Action cards to charge rent, swap cards, demand birthday
               money and lots more. Make sure you put lots of money into your personal
                 bank – and remember other players will steal from you too!
        SET UP
                     1. Remove the 4 Quick Start Rules cards from the pack and hand them out
                         for reference.
                     2. Shuffle the rest of the cards together and deal 5 to each player, face down.
                     3. Look at your cards, but keep them a secret!
                     4. Put the remaining cards face down in the center to create the draw pile.
                     5. Decide who goes first. Play continues clockwise.
