# Research evidence

Local research material only; exclude this directory from production builds and public asset bundles.

- `source-manifest.json`: original PDF hashes, reference commit and review method.
- `monopoly-deal-page-1.png`: complete supplied booklet render.
- `instructions-page-1.png` through `instructions-page-5.png`: supplementary guide renders.
- `*-text.txt`, `monopoly-deal-reading-order.txt`: complete text extractions. The foldout's sorted text has poor column order; visual booklet reading and the unsorted extract were used together.
- `site-instructions-front.png`, `site-instructions-back.png`: photographs linked by the user-requested instructionbook page. Filenames follow the site; identify content by the pictured heading.
- `site-*-property-card.png`, `site-*-wildcard-card.png`, `site-*-rent-card.png`: photographs from `https://monopolydealrules.com/photos/cards/`, linked by its Cards page. Keep the site's misspelled `railraod` filenames for traceability.
- `site-contact-properties.png`, `site-contact-wildcards.png`, `site-contact-rents.png`: local inspection sheets assembled from those photographs. No generated card art.
- `reference-assets.csv`: exhaustive physical media inventory of the reference checkout, including hashes and hold status.
- `dependency-license-evidence.csv`: direct dependency declarations and locally recorded npm-lock license metadata; not a transitive SBOM.

Source pages were accessed 29 September 2026. Rights to republish these photographs, PDF renders or extracted source text have not been established. They are not proposed CardPlay assets. Official source links and relevant page numbers are in the main documents.
